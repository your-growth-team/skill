# Case library

Condensed before/after teardowns that the playbook was built from. Use them as worked examples of the reasoning, especially for the category you're auditing. Each lists what was wrong, the key moves, and the lesson. "Slip" notes record mistakes in the original mockups that the guardrails now prevent; don't repeat them.

**Premium coffee (Hawaiian Kona, $29.95).** Wrong: plain packshot; long "Flavor Profile" paragraph before the CTA; size and type merged in a radio list; subscription buried in a grey box with one-time pre-selected. Moves: blend label above the title; emotional 2-line description; price with weight; pour-over lifestyle hero with a roast meter on the image; six ≤3-word bullets in two columns; ground/whole-bean photo tiles; one-time and subscribe as equal cards with three subscription benefits ("cancel anytime"); service USPs under the CTA; short brand paragraph left visible; FAQs as questions ("Is Kona coffee for you?"); heritage/origin icon grid. Lesson: premium positioning raises the stakes; persuasive copy stays visible, reference copy collapses. Slip: generic USPs ("Gourmet Flavor Experience") and a date mismatch on a badge.

**Drugstore skincare cleanser (₹335).** Wrong: price, reviews and the CTA absent from the first two screens; the good proof ("94% felt gently cleansed", "for dry to normal sensitive skin") buried in a paragraph; broken dropdown label; vertical icon list. Moves: concern pills; benefit line; price with ml; 94% stat moved onto the hero; educational thumbnails; 3 one-line bullets; size cards with "10% off" on the larger; delivery time under the CTA; icon row; "Complete your routine" with numbered steps, pre-checked items and a combined "Add 2 items | ₹710" button; shipping info bar ("free for prepaid orders above ₹999"); MRP line kept. Lesson: relocate before you invent. Slip: added anti-ageing bullets and an "acne-prone" tag not in the source.

**Women's multimineral syrup (US, $74.95).** Already decent page. Moves: form and flavour into the title ("…Supplement Syrup… – Berry Flavor"); audience in the description ("women 18+") replacing an ambiguous segment control; "887 ml (30 days supply)" next to price; BNPL by the price; "See nutritional values" link; size cards with supply duration and purpose labels ("Best Results", "Travel Friendly"); "Best results in 30 days" under the CTA; subscription benefits as a 2×2 icon grid; question accordions; UGC video row; promo-code bar replaced with "Buy more, save more | Build your bundle". Lesson: a must-know layer comes before persuasion; good pages need gap-filling, not rebuilding. Slip: review count changed; HSA/FSA eligibility dropped; dosage hidden in an accordion.

**Mac and cheese 8-pack.** Wrong: huge title, description and disclaimer between gallery and flavour dropdown; single-option pack-size selector. Moves: "Pack of 8" above the title; tagline only; underlined reviews; price above the image; "Most re-ordered" badge; headline nutrition bullets + "See Nutritional Facts"; "1. Choose your flavor" image grid with a variety pack second; "2. Choose your frequency" with per-box prices; dead selector and "Write a review" removed. Lesson: keep the gallery adjacent to the options. Slip: replaced a product-recall notice with a shipping message.

**Protein pastries.** Wrong: brand-led orange page with no product title (a flavour dropdown posing as a heading), "protein" absent from the first fold, no visible price, greyed-out subscription. Moves: title with flavour, form and differentiator ("Frosted Strawberry Protein Pastries"); description leading with "High protein"; price with count; nutrition panel as the peeking second image; three echoed selectors ("Select Flavour: Frosted Strawberry"); variety pack; visible subscription; solid CTA; "Delivery in 2–3 days · Try risk free"; differentiator card and FAQs. Lesson: branding can't carry the page; answer fit, difference and value. Brand colour on the edges, neutral decision zone. Slip: inconsistent pack counts and prices.

**Dark-spot serum ($68).** Wrong: full-bleed model hero pushing everything down; long centred paragraph; free-gift offer buried in text; one-time/subscribe placed below the CTA. Moves: free-samples bar; packshot hero with press badge and clinical stat bar; benefit + mechanism bullets in two columns; free-gift card with value near the CTA; positioning headline ("Finally, a dark spot serum made for our complexions"); routine cross-sell for 10% off. Lesson: the hero answers the top doubt (efficacy here); name the shopper's history with failed alternatives. Slip: review count changed; subscription and Klarna disappeared.

**Sunscreen gel (India, ₹837).** Wrong: everything collapsed into six accordions; pipe-separated USP string; invisible discount. Moves: objection pills ("No white cast", "Non-sticky"); technical description ("four photostable filters, not fillers"); "Save 40%" pill; lifestyle hero proving no white cast with a stat; benefit + mechanism bullets; bundle as a version option; free-sample threshold just above the item price; verified review under the CTA; UGC. Lesson: "too early" means readiness unmet, not pixel position. Slip: dropped the K-beauty origin.

**Olive-oil shots ($77.59 subscription).** Wrong: maroon everywhere, so the maroon CTA blended into a maroon subscription card. Moves: new CTA colour; neutral text; per-day pricing on supply tiles; echoed labels; fixed cadence text instead of a dropdown; before→after testimonial under the CTA; delivery estimate moved up. Lesson: CTA colour scarcity. Slips: low-contrast CTA; new health claims and a "+79% less bloating" stat; invented "free gift every month"; dropped serving size.

**Detox shampoo ($32).** Wrong: CTA literally first, before any fit or benefit; tiny dropdowns. Moves (15 listed): concern badges, 1–2 line description, underlined reviews, value per use ("lasts 60 applications"), best-seller badge, customer-result stat, size cards, highlighted subscription (default), service USPs, icon strip, hero-ingredient card (apple cider vinegar), bullets before accordions, regime cross-sell with bundle price. Lesson: proof ladder; first-fold failure thresholds (bounce > 70%, ATC < 10%, drop-off after CTA). Slips: headline price was the subscription price with wrong maths; scent story dropped.

**SPF moisturiser (Jumbo).** Wrong: two competing promos at the top; five unlabelled image options; red sale price. Moves: gift offer into the bar, code shrunk to a strip; spec-led description; two labelled size cards; priced subscription line; "Works well with" cross-sell. Lesson: the five-zone priority framework (gallery, copy, options, upsell/cross-sell, CTA area) with AOV judged against conversion. Slip: size cards lost prices and "Jumbo & save".

**Cycle multivitamin stick sachets ($64 sub).** Moves: CTA colour removed from the bar; USP badges above the title; title with form and audience ("Multivitamin Stick Sachet for Women"); price near the name; hero showing the sachets; 3 bullets longest→shortest; reduced subscription card with cycle-matched shipping; expert quote under the CTA; quiz kept (resolves fit). Lesson: copy-density thresholds; the hero leaves nothing to assume. Slips: demoted "OBGYN formulated / third-party tested"; dropped a charity line; changed review count and expert.

**Relaxation tea (cans).** Moves: "0g Caffeine · 0g Sugar"; flavour and pack in the title; per-can price at top and on cards; best-seller claim on the hero; variety pack replacing the 48-pack; "When to drink?" accordion; removed false "Limited time". Lesson: bullet placement depends on visual variants; action-verb labels. Slips: removed an auto-renewal disclosure and express checkout; moved the best-seller label.

**Coffee with iconic packaging ($16.99).** Moves: same coffee template; dead size selector folded into "(12oz Bag)"; one-time and subscribe equally legible; subscription framed around free shipping (below the threshold); store-map link removed; brand story condensed. Lesson: category templates are reusable; iconic packaging can be the hero; CTA neither too early nor too late. Slips: duplicated tasting notes; flattened the brand's voice; K-cup advertised on the bag but not selectable.

**Gut-health powder (AU$89.99).** Wrong: two stacked bars; giant cart button; five-line headline filling the first fold. Moves: one social-proof bar; concern pills; flavour and form in the title; "For 240g – 24 doses"; arrow + peek; one-time/subscribe tabs; jar packs labelled "For Best Results" / "Best Value". Lesson: title ≤ 2–3 lines and ~24px; the first two folds are prime real estate. Slips: "Weight Loss" claim introduced; "Best Value" not true across combinations.

**Lip plump ($19).** Moves: title, rating, description and price above the image so shade swatches sit directly under the gallery; shade name + description; "Why you'll love it" after the CTA; large customer-video tiles; shade-guide accordion. Lesson: three-second differentiation for ad traffic in crowded categories. Slips: dropped the ml; dropped claim asterisks; selected swatch ≠ label.

**Men's shampoo with on-page AI assistant ($38).** Moves: smaller title; mechanism-led description (peptides); thumbnails replacing "1/7"; bullets from tab text; tabs → question accordions with a visible truncated "What is it?"; AI suggestion chips re-seeded toward bundles and the top doubt; conditioner cross-sell. Lesson: the first fold must get people to read, click and scroll. Slips: removed "2-order minimum" while adding "cancel anytime"; removed chat-recording consent; CTA matched the bar colour; dropped store pickup and fragrance notes.

**Inspired-by fragrance (India, ₹399–1,299, audited with this skill).** Wrong: the inspired-by promise in small italics; Extrait concentration, 7h longevity, projection and occasion locked in an infographic image; a 15-tier scrolling price ticker; a 3-hour countdown contradicting the cart's 3-day one; no price per ml (unit-price field empty); large note photos and an engraving box pushing the button down; two competing solid buttons; an empty "Scent Notes" accordion; only two gallery images. Moves: inspired-by tag first; descriptor line with "Extrait de Parfum"; spec bar (7H · Strong · Night) as the signature element; size tiles with per-ml prices and "try the 20 ml or tester pack first" for a non-returnable product; bundle and prepaid offers as text; compact engraving; one black primary button with UPI checkout as outlined secondary; scent pyramid; question accordions with the refund policy kept verbatim; a seven-image brief instead of reused photos; mobile and desktop renders. Lesson: for blind-buy categories, the low-risk first try and concrete performance facts do the work reviews would otherwise do.

**Diamond ring ($3,000, multi-visit).** Moves: returns/exchanges bar; spec line under the title; design description; on-hand images; "What's the difference?" for lab vs natural; "Not sure? We'll change it for you" at the size selector; secondary outlined "Talk to us" video CTA; "Made to order · 180-day warranty · Easy returns"; story/specs/warranty/care accordions; no cross-sell. Lesson: high-ticket shifts value to specs and trust to risk reversal and access. Slips: static specs/price that wouldn't update for "Natural"; no lead time.

**Shampoo + conditioner combos ($30).** Wrong: ~30 gallery dots; paragraph; 12 near-identical combo tiles. Moves: scent and benefit in the title; free-from icons; Shark Tank badge; benefit + mechanism bullets; "1M+ … made the switch" proof block; question accordions incl. "Which formula is right for me?"; video row. Lesson: overwhelm = long paragraphs, > 3 options, uneducational images. Better alternative than cutting to one product: decompose 12 combos into "hair need" × "pairing". Slip: lost the combo-saving message.

**Food-storage dish ($55).** Moves: safety pills (non-toxic, plastic-free); spec-led description; tinted panel grouping title and gallery; pack options before the CTA; distinct orange-red CTA; Shop Pay kept as outlined secondary. Lesson: badges + packs before the CTA + CTA in the second fold. Slips: invented pack prices without savings; dropped a size comparison.

**Rattan floor lamp (India, ₹5,999).** Moves: larger image first (aesthetic exception); clean descriptive title; post-code price ("Get for ₹4,799 · Use code"); tick highlights; "From the collection" cross-sell with quick add. Lesson: AOV can be easier to raise than conversion; cross-sell logic matches category. Slips: doubled the discount and lowered its threshold; added free shipping; hid dimensions; removed payment-trust strip.

**Ergonomic chair ($400).** Moves: purpose in the name; founder credential + technology in the description; award badge on a workspace lifestyle hero; 3 bullets; three echoed selectors with a size-guide video; honest "2 left" at variant level; add-on delta inline; trial and shipping under the CTA. Lesson: variant selector must share a screen with the image; information-first ordering for functional purchases. Slip: escalated "relieving back pain" to "pain-free".
